Free · Private · Runs on your PC

Know what you own, and exactly where it is.

Smart Home Inventory is a small desktop app for cataloguing everything in your home — room by room, shelf by shelf. No account, no cloud, no subscription. Everything lives in a single file on your own computer.

Download for Windows See how it works

Built for people who like their home organized

Whether you're moving, insuring your belongings, or just tired of forgetting where you put things.

Home → Room → Zone → Item

Structure your inventory the way you actually think about your space — down to the specific drawer or shelf an item lives on.

Visual room layout

Drag zones around a canvas that mirrors your real room, so finding something is as easy as glancing at a map.

Search everything, instantly

Type an item's name and jump straight to the room and zone it's stored in — across every home you've catalogued.

100% local & private

No sign-up, no tracking, no server. Your inventory is a single file on your own PC that only you can see.

Download

Free for personal use. Windows 10/11, 64-bit.

Download for Windows v1.0.0 · ~100 MB

The installer isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may show a warning the first time you run it. Click "More info" → "Run anyway" to continue — this is normal for small, independently-published apps.

After installing

  1. Open Smart Home Inventory from your Start menu.
  2. Add your first home, then rooms, then zones.
  3. The in-app tour (tap the ? button) walks you through the rest.

Changelog
